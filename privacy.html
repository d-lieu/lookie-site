Lookie privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Lookie is a lookbook app for saving outfits and sharing mood boards with friends. This policy explains what Lookie collects, where it's kept and how to have it deleted.

What stays on your phone

Your looks, outfit photos, colours, captions, binders and boards are kept only on your device. Cutting yourself out of a photo happens on your device too. Lookie doesn't upload any of this unless you choose to post a board.

What Lookie stores if you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, Lookie stores:

  • your Google account's email address and account ID, used only to sign you in;
  • the handle you choose, plus a display name and picture if you add them;
  • who you follow and who follows you;
  • boards you post: the board's picture, name, size and when it was posted;
  • which posted boards you've liked.

This is stored with Supabase, which hosts Lookie's servers. Google sign-in is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

Who can see it

Your handle, display name, picture, follows and likes are visible to other signed-in Lookie users. Boards you post can be seen by anyone with a link to them. Your email address is never shown to other users.

What Lookie doesn't do

Lookie has no ads, doesn't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing, and doesn't use analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

You can delete any board you've posted from inside the app. To delete your whole account, email d.lieu93@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. Your account, profile, follows, posted boards and likes will be deleted within 30 days. Uninstalling the app deletes everything kept on your phone.

Children

Lookie isn't intended for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
